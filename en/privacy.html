Privacy notice

Updated 2026-11-05 · Applies to the current TestFlight public beta

In short: Calotype doesn’t connect to the internet. It doesn’t collect, upload or sell any of your data. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your photos are processed and stored on your iPhone.

Network

The app makes no network requests at all: it doesn’t contact any server, doesn’t upload photos or originals, and doesn’t send usage data. Shooting and developing both happen on the phone.

Camera

Used to capture Apple ProRAW with the rear camera, only while you have the app open to frame and shoot.

Photo library

Used to save developed photos, plus the ProRAW originals if you choose to keep them, to your photo library.

The app asks for read and write access for one reason: when you delete a photo in the app, it also deletes the copy it previously added to your library, and iOS asks you to confirm first. The app only acts on items it added itself. It doesn’t browse or read any other photos in your library.

You can grant limited access or decline. If you decline, photos are still kept in the app; they just aren’t added to your library.

Whether photos in your library sync to iCloud depends on your system settings, not on this app.

Device motion

Used for the level in the viewfinder. The app reads device orientation live to show whether the frame is level. Nothing is recorded or stored.

Location

The current version doesn’t ask for location permission and doesn’t read your location.

Data kept on your phone

  • Originals, developed photos and a technical record of each development (dimensions, timing and similar) are kept in the app’s own folder. You can find it under “On My iPhone” in the Files app.
  • App settings are stored on the device.
  • Deleting the app deletes these files. Photos already saved to your library are not affected.

Sharing and importing

Only when you tap Share does the app hand the files you selected to the iOS share sheet; you decide where they go. Files you pick yourself in the system file picker are read on the device only.

TestFlight

The beta is installed through Apple’s TestFlight. TestFlight may give the developer testing information such as device model, system version, install and session counts, crash reports, and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send. Apple handles this under Apple’s privacy policy; it doesn’t go through the Calotype app itself.

Changes and contact

If a future version changes any of the above, for example by asking for a new permission, this page will be updated first.

Questions are welcome on GitHub Issues.
